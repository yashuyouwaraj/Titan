# Titan AI — Research and References

---

# Purpose

This document serves as the central repository for all research materials, technical references, academic papers, books, articles, datasets, benchmarks, and engineering resources used throughout the Titan AI project.

It provides a structured knowledge base that supports the design, implementation, and continuous improvement of Titan AI.

This document is continuously updated as new research is studied.

# Research Principles

Every research item included in this document should contribute to one or more of the following:

- Understanding existing techniques
- Improving Titan AI
- Solving engineering problems
- Exploring new architectures
- Validating implementation decisions
- Identifying future research opportunities
